Vanguard Shift

Command your squad from above, then drop into any soldier and fight in first person. Possessed soldiers deal double damage. Destroy the red command post.

Desktop

  • Left click / drag select units (Shift adds)
  • Right click move, or attack an enemy
  • X then click: attack-move · Q select all
  • WASD / arrows, middle-drag pan · wheel zoom
  • F or double-click a unit: possess
  • In FPS: WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, R rally squad, E swap, F/Tab back

Touch

  • Tap a unit to select · drag to box-select
  • With units selected, tap ground to move, an enemy to attack
  • Two fingers pan and pinch-zoom
  • Double-tap a unit or press Possess
  • In FPS: left stick moves, drag right side to aim, hold FIRE
