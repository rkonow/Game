🦆 Fowl Play 🥚

Ducks vs. Hens: the Great Egg War

The hens hid behind a giant eggshell. Sit on the three nests A B C in the middle to crack it, then flatten their coop. Only a duck you control in first person can claim a nest.

Commander (top view)

  • Move your flock to guard the nests
  • Spend 🌽 corn on birds: Duck, Goose, Heron. Every nest you hold pays more corn
  • Desktop: click / drag to select, right click to move or attack, 1 2 3 buy, F or double-click to drop in
  • Touch: tap / drag to select, tap ground to move, two fingers pan and zoom, double-tap to drop in

Duck mode (first person)

  • Sit on a nest to claim it · you deal x2 damage
  • Desktop: WASD + mouse, click to fire, Q/E abilities, X ultimate, R rally, C swap, F/Tab back
  • Touch: left stick, drag right side to aim, hold FIRE
  • If your duck gets cooked, wait 5s to drop in again

Duck: egg bomb, dive roll, ★ duckling swarm · Goose: wing wall, charge, ★ the HONK · Heron: scope, stilt jump, ★ hawk eye · Watch out for the Big Rooster · Alerts have a Drop in button that takes you straight there

Real-time: command and drop in whenever you like. Hens attack in waves.

Turn-based: the battle freezes between 20-second live rounds. Re-arrange your flock, buy birds, upgrade and build towers, then play one ★ hero live. Hold all 3 nests to crack the coop, then destroy it.
