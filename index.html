HOW TO PLAY

The swarm's hive hides behind a bio-shield. Hold the three relays A B C in the middle to bring it down, then destroy the hive. Only a marine you control in first person can capture a relay.

Commander (top view)

  • Move your squad to guard the relays. Drag from a marine to where it should go
  • Spend 💠 ore to deploy a Marine, Juggernaut or Ranger. Every relay you hold pays more ore
  • Desktop: click / drag to select, right click to move or attack, 1 2 3 deploy, F or double-click to drop in
  • Touch: tap / drag to select, tap ground to move, two fingers pan and zoom, double-tap to drop in

First person

  • Stand on a relay to capture it · you deal x2 damage
  • Desktop: WASD + mouse, click to fire, Q/E abilities, X ultimate, right mouse aims, R reload, G rally, C swap, F/Tab back
  • Touch: left stick, drag right side to aim, hold FIRE
  • If your marine goes down, wait 5s to drop in again

Campaign is turn-based: each marine gets 2 actions (move, dash, shoot, overwatch, hunker, grenade). Once per turn you can Drop in and play one marine live in first person for 20 seconds, then it's back to turns. Cover on the shooter's side lowers the hit chance; flank aliens to crit them. Then the swarm takes its turn. Dead aliens drop loot: ore, medkits, ult cells and rare relics your marine keeps.

Marine: frag grenade, combat roll, ★ drone swarm · Juggernaut: energy wall, shield charge, ★ shockwave · Ranger: scope, jump jet, ★ recon scan · Watch out for the Hive Tyrant · Alerts have a Drop in button that takes you straight there
