Vanguard Shift

Command from above, then drop into any soldier. The command view only sees what your units see, and the red post is shielded: only a soldier you control can plant a charge to drop it. Possessing burns Overdrive (x2 damage), which recharges while you command.

Desktop

  • Left click / drag select units (Shift adds)
  • Right click move, or attack an enemy
  • X then click: attack-move · Q select all
  • WASD / arrows, middle-drag pan · wheel zoom
  • F or double-click a unit: possess
  • 1 / 2 / 3 artillery, cover, reinforce (command points)
  • In FPS: WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, Q role ability, T mark target, G plant charge, R rally squad, E swap, F/Tab back

Touch

  • Tap a unit to select · drag to box-select
  • With units selected, tap ground to move, an enemy to attack
  • Two fingers pan and pinch-zoom
  • Double-tap a unit or press Possess
  • Power buttons on the right: artillery, cover, reinforce
  • In FPS: left stick moves, drag right side to aim, hold FIRE, plus Ability and Mark

Roles · Trooper: frag grenade · Heavy: armor up · Scout: dash, long sight · Sniper: scope, headshots · Medic: heals, revives fallen allies
